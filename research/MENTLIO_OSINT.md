# Mentlio public-surface research

Research date: **2026-08-27**. Scope: public, unauthenticated web pages and downloadable assets only.

## Executive finding

Mentlio currently positions itself as engineering intelligence and AI cost/productivity infrastructure, not as a customer-feedback product. Its public story connects engineering-delivery signals with AI-tool activity and adds token/cost controls for local coding agents.

The clean-room competitor opportunity is to make the management decision more transparent and safer: team/workflow analysis, evidence receipts, explicit uncertainty, and a reversible experiment instead of an individual leaderboard or opaque productivity score.

## Observed public product surface

- Overview, productivity, leaderboard, and savings views.
- Delivery/quality framing and AI-assisted engineering activity.
- A local desktop agent for common coding-agent environments.
- Token-oriented surfaces publicly named Route, Lens, Quiet, Logs, and Recall.
- Public references to GitHub, Jira, Linear, Slack, Teams, Claude Code, OpenAI/Codex, and Cursor.
- Documentation, research, security, status, changelog, and downloadable pilot installer surfaces.
- No public self-serve price was observed during this capture.

These observations describe public positioning, not a reverse-engineered implementation. Authenticated functionality and proprietary formulas were not accessed.

## Clean-room rules applied

- Independent name, logo, color system, typography, copy, information architecture, and fictional dataset.
- No competitor screenshot or downloaded asset in `web/public` or application imports.
- No reproduction of proprietary formulas, benchmark claims, customer claims, or authenticated behavior.
- Product claims are limited to behavior implemented and tested in this repository.
- Demo metrics are visibly identified as fictional associations rather than customer outcomes or causal proof.
